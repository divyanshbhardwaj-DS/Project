Core Thesis

Every day, tens of millions of Indian commuters and students rely on shared e-rickshaws. However, the entire system operates on pure tribal guesswork: no published schedules, no seat visibility, and no arrival forecasts.

Sarthi is not another ride-hailing dispatcher (like Uber or Ola). Sarthi is a lightweight, real-time visibility layer over the existing shared transit network.

Selected Name: SARTHI

Derived from the ancient Hindi word for ‘guide / charioteer’ — evoking trust, direction, companionship, and safety. Tested against 10 contenders (RickPulse, RouteMitra, Setu Mobility, GaadiTrack) and selected for cross-linguistic memorability.

“Sarthi doesn’t create new rides — it makes the rides that already exist visible, predictable, and efficient.”

The Real-world Triangle of Waste

  • For Passengers: Uncertainty, wasted wait times (10–25 mins), unnecessary 400m+ walks to arbitrary stops, and safety anxiety for women/students.
  • For Drivers: Battery/fuel drained roaming empty streets searching for passengers, unstable daily income, low trips-per-hour.
  • For Cities: Needless congestion, horn pollution, and lack of any transit telemetry for Smart City planners.

Why Existing Solutions Fail

Alternative Why It Fails for Shared E-Rickshaws
Uber / Ola / Rapido Built for single-passenger private dispatch with high 20–25% commissions. Destroys shared ₹10 unit economics.
WhatsApp Groups Unstructured, noisy, doesn't provide real-time GPS radar or seat counters.
Hardware GPS Trackers Prohibitive hardware & SIM maintenance cost for informal driver-owners.

Tech Stack Architecture

Frontend: React Native + Ultra-lightweight PWA (Runs on low-spec phones)
Backend: Node.js / NestJS + Socket.io (Low-latency concurrent location streams)
Database: PostgreSQL + PostGIS (Sub-second geospatial 'nearby' bounding queries)
Real-time Cache: Redis Pub/Sub (Geo-caching live rickshaw locations)
AI / ML Core: FastAPI + Scikit/PyTorch (ETA prediction, unsupervised GPS trail clustering)
Inclusion Layer: SMS & USSD Gateway (*99*77#) for non-smartphone commuters

Concrete AI Capabilities

  • AI ETA Engine: Time-series on historical trip durations + time-of-day traffic.
  • Unsupervised Route Ingestion: Automatically groups driver GPS breadcrumbs into informal route corridors without manual surveys.
  • Smart Stop Matching: Minimizes [Wait Time + Walking Time] combined.
  • Safety Anomaly Engine: Detects route deviations or unexpected long stops.

Multi-Layer Monetization (No Heavy Commission)

Since e-rickshaw margins are thin, Sarthi avoids heavy driver commissions and unlocks high-margin digital layers:

1. Passenger Convenience Safe-Pass: ₹1–₹2 optional booking-assurance & SOS link fee for confirmed seats.
2. Driver Pro Subscription: ₹99/month for advanced demand heatmaps, earnings tracker, and priority placement.
3. Hyperlocal Sponsored Pins: Local food stalls, coaching institutes, and shops display geo-targeted promos along routes.
4. Smart City Transit Data Licensing: Anonymized last-mile mobility feeds licensed to municipal transport authorities.
5. College & School Fleet Safe-Ride Portal: Parent-facing safety dashboards with live tracking contracts.
